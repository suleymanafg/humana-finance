# Business context — read before touching anything

This app is the financial + supply-planning system for **Humana Uzbekistan**: Turbo Impex LLC (TI, the owner's importer) distributes Humana baby formula (DMK Baby, Bremen, Germany). The user is the owner/admin. Working language is Russian; the UI is RU-first bilingual.

## Company structure & the Fargo deal (critical to interpret any number)
- **Turbo Impex (TI)** imports from Germany (always PREPAYS — TI never owes Germany; «товар в пути» = TI's advances). TI pays 15% profit tax.
- **Fargo** is the distributor — a 50/50 partner. On paper TI "sells" to Fargo at COGS+3–10% (transfer price), but economically it is **consignment**: Fargo sells at the app's retail prices and must remit ALL collections (cash + bank) minus its own expenses. The app's settlement model («Осталось за Fargo») IS the real contract; the paper price and акт сверки are meaningless for economics. Fargo pays 12% VAT + 1.9% turnover tax (modeled in the engine).
- Fargo distributes other brands too, so it CANNOT report brand-level cash. The settlement receivable is verifiable only through the app's model.
- Korzinka (~35% of revenue) is on fast payment in exchange for extra discounts; since April 2026 the discount is **booked as Fargo OPEX** (category «Korzinka финансирование», ≈6% of Korzinka revenue), so the settlement receivable is already net of it. Revenue itself is still qty × справочник price − retro %; the cleaner permanent fix remains the 1C invoiced-amounts field («Сумма выручки с НДС»).

## Data policies
- **pinetrade 1C is the source of truth for sales** (qty only; `Sale.amount` is null for synced rows → engine values revenue at Settings prices). Sell-in (channel orders), NOT consumer sell-out — monthly numbers sawtooth.
- Promo SKUs (АКЦИЯ) are separate products linked via `regularProductId`; planning/costing folds them into the regular physical SKU.
- **Month close**: closed months (Month.closedAt) are frozen for STAFF and 1C feeds; only ADMIN edits/reopens. The P&L shows closed months only.
- Balance sheet: stock at avg TI landed cost; «Не сверено» (plug) is the reconciliation error — only MEASURED lines (stock counts, банк/касса TI, AR, transit) move it; modeled lines (settlement) cannot.
- Stock: monthly month-end counts (StockCount); a daily 1C push to `/api/import/1c-stock` is planned but not yet scheduled by the 1C dev.

## Baby-formula demand mechanics (drives the planning module)
- Demand is driven by **medical detailing**: med reps get doctors to prescribe; each verified prescription (foil tracking) = one recruited baby. ~3 packs/month if fully retained; observed retention ≈35–45% blended, falling with age.
- **Stages**: Platin 1 (baby age 0–6 мес) → Platin 2 (6–12) → Platin 3 (12+, fading). Today's P1 recruitment predicts P2 demand ~6 months later (the "wave"). Expert line (AC/AR/HN/SL) is specialty — stable, not cohort-driven.
- The cohort model lives in `src/lib/planning/cohort.ts` + `model.ts`: recruitment × retention curve × packs, calibrated per stage against recent sell-in (hybrid: multiplicative where the programme dominates, additive base where pre-programme demand exists). Recruitment series in PlanningRecruitment (owner enters monthly verified Rx).
- Births in Uzbekistan are falling −3…5%/yr; growth must come from share (2027 plan: 340k units vs 220k in 2026).

## Supply chain (planning module)
- Orders are entered into Humana's **IBP system by the 20th of each month** for the ship month 4 months later (the IBP for ship month M closes on the 20th of M − 4). A truck is sellable **45 days after dispatch** from DMK (owner, Oct 2026); a sellable date after the 15th counts from the next month. Committed purchases (PlanningPurchase, per SKU per SHIP month) come from DMK's IBP export; contracts from order forms are historical seeds only.
- **HQ's truck** (the order form's capacity block): 33 places; a pallet takes layers per pallet ÷ layers per place of its load category (PlanningSku.loadCategory: 1 milks/standards 10, 2 cereals 9, 3 desserts 14, 4 400 g Platin 15) — 0.8 for Platin/800 g/HN/SL, 1.2 for AC/AR. HQ: each truck ≥ 0.98 (below = transport at the buyer's risk) and the form must show 0.98 → with 0.8/1.2 pallets every truck is exactly **32.4 places, with an odd number of AC/AR pallets**. Whole pallets only.
- **Truck statuses** (Shipment.status): PLANNED (agreed load, not ordered) → ORDERED → TRANSIT → CUSTOMS → ARRIVED. Only ARRIVED is in stock, FIFO and COGS (`costing.ts`); the rest are supply planning's pipeline, sellable from etaDate (expected sellable date; the actual day once arrived), else dispatch + 45 days. A non-arrived truck's monthId is its dispatch month until «Mark arrived» moves it. The Shipments page lists every non-arrived truck with a status switch; the overview's «в пути» shows TRANSIT + CUSTOMS.
- The section, rebuilt Oct 2026 from the owner's v17 workbook (`plan-data.ts` → `loadPlan`, shaped for screens by `plan-view.ts`):
  - **Demand — the stage engine** (`stage-engine.ts`): babies on a stage = verified prescriptions (PlanningRecruitment, from the foil tracking file) of the last 6 months by age (P1 0–5, P2 6–11, P3 12–17); stage demand = level + tins per baby × babies; the level is learned from the last 3 closed clean months (a month that started with stock < ½ of sales is "short", not clean); 800 g share from the latest clean month; Expert SKUs at their own 3-month level; the current month = sold so far + the rest of the month at the forecast. Prescriptions ahead = average of the last 3 unless set.
  - **Orders — the planner** (`order-planner.ts`, split by `truck-split.ts`): per ship month, the load that leaves stock at the end of its sell month at the target months of cover (TARGET by ship month; default in settings); open quantities at DMK are collected first; ship months up to the IBP deadline that passed take only what is in the IBP, except the pick-up horizon (this month + `pickupMonths`) where HQ can be asked for more; whole pallets in whole trucks at 32.4 places; a spill under a quarter truck is trimmed from products that stay above the danger line. Output per ship month: units, trucks, fill, the IBP number to enter (load − what comes from open at DMK), €.
  - Month-end stock is the Stock page's (computed FIFO TI + Fargo units); "open at DMK" = IBP ordered for ship months up to this one − trucks by dispatch month since 2026-01 (any status), optionally corrected by OPEN_FIX (stored as the difference to the app's count).
  - The owner's say: `PlanningInput` (kind SHORT stock-out mark per SKU+month, OWN_DEMAND own monthly number per SKU, OWN_SHARE own 800 g share per stage, TARGET months of cover per ship month, OPEN_FIX) via `/api/planning/inputs`; engine settings in Setting `planning.engine` via `/api/planning/settings`.
  - Pages: `/planning` «Этот месяц» (pick-ups this and next month with the truck split, «create as planned trucks» → `/api/planning/trucks`, HQ order form fill → `/api/planning/hq-form`; the IBP numbers due by the 20th vs what is entered; trucks not arrived; cover by month), `/planning/orders` «Заказы» (15-month grid by ship month: IBP to enter vs now / truck loads; below it the IBP export upload and the entered orders), `/planning/demand` «Спрос» (prescriptions + foil upload `/api/planning/foil-import`, stage levels, sales vs forecast with clickable stock-out marks, own numbers), `/planning/settings` «Настройки».
  - **HQ's order form** (`hq-form.ts`): the owner uploads HQ's latest form, the app fills L/M/N per truck (each product's contract lines in row order up to their open qty J, the rest on its last line or a new line under «order without contract confirmation»), rewrites the Mapping Truck 1–3 formulas (HQ's copies lose rows to #REF! or double-count a line) and sets recalculation on open. It edits the XML cell by cell — an ExcelJS rewrite leaves a file Excel will not open. HQ's form is never stored (public repo).
  - `buildPlan({ addTrucks })` runs the plan with trucks not yet in the app (what-if; nothing written).
- The old screens' logic (`supply.ts`, `SupplyView`) is no longer on a page; `data.ts` (`loadPlanning`) still feeds the plan (sales, purchases, open at DMK, recruitment, the IBP slot).
- DMK's IBP export is trusted **only for the orders** ("Partner Forecast Input Qty." per ship month → PlanningPurchase, from a ship month on): its Order History, Contract Volumes and Open Orders are wrong and DMK cannot correct them (owner, 2026-10-13). Upload on «Заказы» (check, then apply) or `prisma/import-ibp-sync.ts <file> [--from YYYY-MM] [--commit]`.
- Every PlanningSku needs its `productId` link or it shows no stock, sales or trucks — `prisma/link-planning-skus.ts` restores the links (dry run; `--commit` writes).
- Germany renumbered articles; PlanningSku.altArticles maps old→new (70836→70987 etc.).

## Operational conventions
- Dev DB = Neon `dev` branch (in .env); production = `main` branch (commented `# DATABASE_URL_PRODUCTION=` line — never print its value). Vercel auto-deploys master; **schema changes need a manual `prisma db push` against production BEFORE pushing code** (the owner runs it in PowerShell 5.1 — no `&&`, one command per line).
- `prisma/debug-*.ts` are gitignored read-only diagnostics (support `--production`). `prisma/refresh-dev-from-prod.ts` mirrors prod→dev (owner runs `--commit`).
- New financial model (branch `claude/upbeat-knuth-bbh4ug`, Vercel preview): since Oct 2026 the owner enters real data into it, and it runs on the Neon `dev` branch. That branch is no longer a disposable copy — never refresh it from production (the script refuses unless `--force`), never reset it from its parent, and remember that `prisma/import-workbook.ts` replaces everything up to the workbook's report month (rows after it stay as the app has them) — re-import only a workbook the owner gives as the corrected record. The plan is a separate Vercel project for this version with its own database once it has proven itself.
- Roles: ADMIN (owner) / STAFF (figures only) / VIEWER. Planning section is ADMIN-only.
- For bulk data the owner provides files (Downloads folder); parse with the `xlsx` npm package (no Python on this machine).

## Distribution handover (/transition) — temporary module
- Plan for moving Humana trade from Fargo into TI (target: new structure from January 2027): tasks by workstream, a row per sales channel (route, switch date, status), outlet register, Fargo's stock by batch for the buyback, monthly sales continuity by channel, open decisions. ADMIN-only, like planning.
- Self-contained: tables `Tr*` (TrRegion, TrTask, TrOutlet, TrStockLot, TrDecision, TrTiSale) point at the rest of the app by plain ids and nothing outside `src/lib/transition`, `src/app/transition`, `src/app/api/transition` and the chat's `transition_*` tools reads them. Existing data is read, never written. Drop the module by deleting those folders, the `tr*` CRUD entries and the `Tr*` models.
- The starting plan (`src/lib/transition/seed-plan.ts`) is loaded once from the Plan page and is idempotent (keyed by `seedKey` / `channelId`). After that the database is the record.
- The AI briefing (Setting `transition.brief`) and go-live date (`transition.goLive`) are edited on the overview; the chat appends the briefing to its system prompt for ADMIN sessions.
- Uploads: `.xlsx` / `.csv` only (exceljs + papaparse); the owner maps columns in the browser, then preview → commit.
